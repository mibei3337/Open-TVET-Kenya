Autobody Technology

KNQF Level: Level 5  |  ISCED Code: 0716 554B  |  Awarding Body: TVET CDACC

Program Overview

Competency Summary

Autobody Technology Level 5 consists of competencies that an individual must achieve to repair and maintain vehicle bodies using environmentally sustainable, resource-efficient, and safety-conscious practices. The qualification integrates green skills, circular economy principles, pollution prevention, and energy efficiency across all auto body operations.

Curriculum Architecture

Modular Structure & Units of Learning

Module I (290 Hours)
  • Vehicle Structure Welding I (90 Hours)
  • Vehicle Body Repair (110 Hours)
  • Vehicle Fibre Works (90 Hours)
Module II (330 Hours)
  • Vehicle Body Surface Preparation (90 Hours)
  • Vehicle Spray Painting (150 Hours)
  • Vehicle Body Valeting (90 Hours)
Module III (440 Hours)
  • Communication Skills (40 Hours)
  • Work Ethics and Practices (40 Hours)
  • Applied Mathematics (80 Hours)
  • Technical Drawing (80 Hours)
  • Vehicle Glass Components Installation (140 Hours)
  • Glazing Finishing Processes (140 Hours)
Module IV (540 Hours)
  • Digital Literacy (40 Hours)
  • Entrepreneurial Skills (40 Hours)
  • Workshop Technology (80 Hours)
  • Mechanical Science (80 Hours)
  • Electronics and Control Principles (80 Hours)
  • Glass Components Maintenance (140 Hours)

Program Specifications

  • Total Duration: 2,080 Hours
  • Total Credit Factor: 208.0
  • Industry Attachment: 480 Hours
  • Assessment: Formative (60%) & Summative (40%)

Minimum Entry Requirements

  • KCSE Mean Grade D Plain, OR
  • KNQF Level 5 qualification certificate in Auto Body Technology or equivalent.
