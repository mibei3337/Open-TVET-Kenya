Analytical Chemistry Technology

KNQF Level: Level 6  |  ISCED Code: 0531 551  |  Awarding Body: TVET CDACC

Program Overview

Competency Summary

The Analytical Chemistry Technology Level 6 consists of competencies that an individual must have to effectively perform duties of an analytical chemistry technician. It involves collecting chemical samples, performing classical analysis techniques, biochemical analysis, instrumental analysis, chemical separation, quality assurance and control, industrial chemistry procedures, and Green Hydrogen production quality control.

Curriculum Architecture

Modular Structure & Units of Learning

  • Chemical Sample Preparation (200 Hours)
  • Chemical Separation (200 Hours)
Module II (480 Hours)
  • Mathematics for Science (150 Hours)
  • Organic Chemistry Principles (150 Hours)
  • Biochemical Techniques (180 Hours)
Module III (440 Hours)
  • Communication Skills (40 Hours)
  • Inorganic Chemistry Principles (150 Hours)
  • Classical Analysis Techniques (250 Hours)
Module IV (400 Hours)
  • Digital Literacy (40 Hours)
  • Physics Principles (150 Hours)
  • Instrumental Analysis (210 Hours)
Module V (415 Hours)
  • Work Ethics and Practices (40 Hours)
  • Physical Chemistry Principles (150 Hours)
  • Industrial Chemistry Analysis (225 Hours)
Module VI (470 Hours)
  • Entrepreneurial Skills (40 Hours)
  • Research Methods (150 Hours)
  • Quality Assurance and Control (180 Hours)
  • Green Hydrogen Production Quality Control (100 Hours)

Program Specifications

  • Total Duration: 3,085 Hours
  • Credit Factor: 309
  • Industry Attachment: 480 Hours
  • Assessment: 60% Practical / 40% Theory

Minimum Entry Requirements

  • KCSE Mean Grade C- (Minus), OR
  • Related Level 5 Certificate, OR
  • Equivalent qualifications determined by TVETA.
