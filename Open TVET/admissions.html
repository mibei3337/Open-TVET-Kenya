Prerequisites

What You Need to Get Started

We keep our entry pathways flexible to accommodate learners from diverse backgrounds across Kenya.

  • National ID or Passport number for student registration
  • KCSE certificate or relevant prior vocational qualification (Levels 3–6)
  • Basic proficiency in English or Kiswahili
  • Access to a smartphone, tablet, or computer with internet connectivity

Regulatory Compliance & Standards

Open TVET Kenya operates in alignment with national technical and vocational education frameworks. For official accreditation guidelines and standards in Kenya, you can consult the TVETA website.
