Who We Are

Redefining Technical & Vocational Learning

Open TVET Kenya is a premier technical and vocational education institution built to break down geographical and scheduling barriers, making industry-aligned CDACC skills training available to everyone.

We recognize that modern careers require more than just theory. Our programs are engineered to prepare students for employment, self-employment, and successful entrepreneurship across diverse technical sectors.

By integrating advanced digital learning management systems with structured workshop sessions, we provide an immersive environment where learners can master both digital concepts and physical techniques.

Our Core Commitment

To bridge the gap between digital accessibility and physical craftsmanship, ensuring every graduate attains true industry readiness and practical competence.

Our Methodology

The Practical Learning Journey

We believe mastering a skill requires a continuous, cyclical process. Understanding a lesson online is only the beginning—true competence is forged through active practice, expert evaluation, and iterative improvement.

Every course across our departments follows this proven framework to guarantee student success.

  • 01
    Learn

    Study core concepts, digital notes, and high-definition expert demonstrations.

  • 02
    Practise

    Apply knowledge through guided hands-on projects and workshop tasks.

  • 03
    Submit Evidence & Feedback

    Upload your practical work securely for detailed professional review.

  • 04
    Demonstrate Competence

    Refine your performance based on feedback to achieve certified mastery.
